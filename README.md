> **Grand Coast Outlaws (GCO)** is an independent, open-world action multiplayer game currently in **early Unreal Engine 5 / C++ prototype development**. All artwork on this page is labelled **concept art**, not actual gameplay. No public playable build or release date has been announced.

## THE WORLD — A CITY WITHOUT SIMPLE CHOICES

Picture a fictional coastal metropolis with dense business districts, nightlife, sunlit beaches, industrial docks, surrounding islands and long roads connecting it all. The **long-term design ambition** is a **30 × 30 km (900 km²)** world. That figure describes a planned scope, **not** an existing or completed map.

The core fantasy is a living online world where police officers, criminal crews and other players operate in the same city. A bank alarm might spark a pursuit; a vehicle escape might cross into the harbor; a single decision might turn a quiet night into a multiplayer standoff. The team is approaching this ambition in smaller, testable milestones.

### Planned world regions

- **Downtown:** business districts, dense city streets and night-time pursuits.
- **Coastline & islands:** beaches, island roads and water-based connections.
- **Harbor & airfield:** industrial infrastructure and future objective locations.

These are setting proposals rather than verified in-game locations. [Media publication status](docs/MEDIA.md).

## THE GAMEPLAY VISION

The following are planned experiences, not a claim that they are complete:

- **Police & law enforcement:** patrols, dispatch-style objectives, pursuit and coordinated responses to crime.
- **Outlaws & crews:** escaping custody, assembling crews, undertaking risky jobs and navigating the consequences of criminal activity.
- **Robberies & heists:** bank, jewelry, retail and other location-based objectives, ultimately connected to police response and reward systems.
- **Vehicles & traversal:** driving, pursuit, roads, coastal routes and a varied vehicle ecosystem.
- **Wanted, bounty & progression:** risk and reward across competing player roles; persistent online systems are a future technical milestone.
- **An evolving city:** a day/night atmosphere, civilian activity, different districts and additional world events as development progresses.

Read the [extended game vision](docs/GAME_VISION.md) for setting, game loops and planned locations.

## ENGINEERING — BUILD THE FIRST DISTRICT FIRST

This is a **public-facing showcase**, separate from the private Unreal Engine development repository. Internal notes last comprehensively reviewed on **6 October 2026** report an initial compiling Unreal editor foundation, early C++ modules, blockout maps, a prototype menu and narrow two-player PIE listen-server tests.

Those reports **do not** demonstrate a completed multiplayer game, finished heists, production-ready dedicated server, completed 900 km² world or stable performance at scale.

## TECHNOLOGY & PARTNERSHIPS

GCO's development direction is **Unreal Engine 5 with C++**. The near-term infrastructure goal is a measured pilot: package a genuine server build, connect independent clients, test replication, and report repeatable CPU, memory and network measurements.

Infrastructure providers and technical partners can review the [partner overview](docs/PARTNER_OVERVIEW.md) and [hosting evaluation brief](docs/HOSTING_EVALUATION.md). A provider is not presented as a confirmed sponsor without an agreement.

## ABOUT BLACKSPUR GAMES

**Blackspur Games / Blackspur Entertainment** is building Grand Coast Outlaws as an ambitious independent game project. The public repository presents its vision, visuals, roadmap and carefully scoped engineering updates. Production code, internal maps and unreleased assets stay private.
