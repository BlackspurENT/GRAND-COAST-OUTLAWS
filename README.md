## The project

**Grand Coast Outlaws (GCO)** imagines a fictional coastal metropolis shaped by police operations, criminal crews and the choices players make together. Neon downtown streets, waterfront districts and routes beyond the city provide the setting for pursuits, high-risk objectives and competing ambitions.

The project is in **early Unreal Engine 5 / C++ prototype development**. The immediate goal is a small playable district with a repeatable multiplayer objective, followed by dedicated-server validation and a focused gameplay vertical slice.

## The gameplay direction

- **Two sides of the law.** Police response and criminal objectives within the same multiplayer setting.
- **A coast with character.** Contrasting downtown, shoreline and industrial districts connected by roads and coastal routes.
- **Movement with consequences.** Vehicles, pursuits and escapes that connect objectives across the world.
- **Shared risk and reward.** Crew coordination, wanted systems and progression as development advances.

These are design goals. The [game vision](docs/GAME_VISION.md) describes the intended experience; the [development status](docs/DEVELOPMENT_STATUS.md) records the prototype work reported so far. The long-term world ambition is separate from the scope of the first playable district.

## Development, with a clear next step

Internal notes last reviewed on **6 October 2026** describe a compiling Unreal Editor foundation, early C++ modules, map blockouts, a prototype menu and limited two-player editor listen-server tests.

The next gates are independent client validation, a packaged dedicated server and reproducible resource measurements. Public gameplay captures will be added when available. This showcase does not claim production multiplayer readiness or a completed open world.
