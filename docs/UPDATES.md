## 6 October 2026 — Internal technical milestone snapshot

Based on project development notes, the team reported:
- Successful Unreal Engine Development Editor compilation.
- Initial coastal-world maps and an early in-engine menu scene.

These results were recorded internally and have **not** been independently reproduced as public deployment benchmarks. Some work may have changed since that snapshot; only reviewed changes are added to this public log.

## How future updates will be published

A meaningful new entry should include:
- Date and area of work.
- What actually changed.
- Evidence (e.g. screenshot from a tested build, build result, bounded benchmark).
- Remaining limitations.
- Whether a milestone is **Done**, **In progress**, **Blocked**, or **Planned**.

Avoid publishing sensitive logs, internal paths, tokens, licensed assets or unauthorised screenshots.
