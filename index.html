About the game

Grand Coast Outlaws (GCO) is an ambitious open-world multiplayer game in development with Unreal Engine 5 and C++. Explore the vision of a sprawling coastal metropolis where police and outlaws collide, with vehicles, pursuits, heists and the freedom to forge your own story.
